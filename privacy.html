Your information and privacy

Privacy Notice

How Neurodivergence Revived CIC handles personal information when you visit our website or contact us.

Last reviewed: Please confirm the publication date before publishing this notice.

1. Who we are

Neurodivergence Revived CIC is a UK Community Interest Company supporting neurodivergent people and their families. Essie Rewane is our primary contact for privacy enquiries.

You can contact us at either of these email addresses:

  • info@neurodivergencerevived.org
  • neurodivergencerevived@gmail.com

This notice explains our intended approach to handling personal information under applicable data-protection law, including the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 where applicable.

2. Information we may receive

Depending on how you contact us, we may receive:

  • Your name and email address.
  • The type of enquiry you select and the services you are interested in.
  • The contents of messages submitted through our contact form, email or WhatsApp.
  • Information relating to professional referrals, volunteering, partnerships or support requests that you choose to share.
  • Technical information that may be recorded by our hosting provider or third-party services, such as IP address, browser or device details, request time and information used to detect spam or abuse.

Information about disability, neurodivergence, health or support needs may be sensitive personal information. Please do not include detailed medical, safeguarding or other sensitive information in a general enquiry or chatbot message. Contact us first to discuss an appropriate way to share it.

3. How we use information

We may use personal information to respond to enquiries, discuss our services, consider referrals and volunteering requests, communicate with families and professionals, coordinate agreed support, maintain appropriate records and protect our website against spam or misuse.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Website forms, email and WhatsApp

The website does not use its own application database to store contact-form submissions. However, information submitted through the form is processed by services used to deliver and protect the form and may be delivered to and retained in our email accounts.

The contact form uses EmailJS and Google reCAPTCHA. These providers may process information under their own privacy terms.

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, your correspondence may remain in the relevant email or messaging account and may be retained in our organisational records where needed.

5. Our website chatbot

Nova, our website chatbot, uses predefined keyword-based responses. In the current website configuration, chatbot messages are not sent to Claude through the chatbot.

Please do not share medical details, referral records, passwords or other sensitive information through the chatbot.

6. Service providers and sharing

We rely on service providers to operate our website and communications. These may include our hosting provider, EmailJS, Google reCAPTCHA, email providers and WhatsApp. External resources such as fonts and icon libraries may also receive technical connection information when your browser loads them.

We share information only where reasonably needed for the purpose described in this notice, where you have asked us to do so, or where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

Where information needs to be shared with another professional or organisation as part of a referral, appropriate sharing arrangements should be discussed with you first, unless a legal or safeguarding obligation requires otherwise.

7. Lawful basis and sensitive information

Applicable data-protection law requires an appropriate lawful basis for processing personal information. Depending on the circumstances, this may include responding to your request, taking steps towards an arrangement you have asked about, our legitimate interests in operating our services, or meeting a legal obligation.

Where special-category information, such as health data, is processed, an additional legal condition and appropriate safeguards may be required. The organisation should confirm and document the relevant basis before collecting or using sensitive referral or support information.

8. International transfers

Some third-party providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where data-protection law requires safeguards for international transfers, those safeguards should be checked and put in place.

9. How long we keep information

We aim to keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, taking account of applicable legal, accounting, safeguarding and record-keeping requirements and the handling of disputes.

The appropriate period depends on the type of record and the circumstances. We should establish and apply a documented retention schedule for enquiries, referrals, volunteer records and service records rather than keep information indefinitely.

10. Your rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions in applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, request erasure or restriction, object to certain processing, or request data portability where applicable.

If processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent. This does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

To make a request, contact Essie Rewane using either email address listed above. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

11. Complaints

Please contact us first if you have concerns about how your information is handled. If you are in the United Kingdom, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

12. Security and changes

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our website and communications. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive information through an unapproved or general-purpose channel.

We may update this notice when our services, systems or legal obligations change. We will review it periodically to ensure that it reflects our actual practices.

Privacy contact: Essie Rewane, Neurodivergence Revived CIC. Email: info@neurodivergencerevived.org or neurodivergencerevived@gmail.com.
